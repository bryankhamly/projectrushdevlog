Direction

A two-game strategy to build the World IP.

Game 1 Builds the IP

Low-scope game, focused on fun

  • Builds a World IP — establishes the fiction and universe.
  • Low scope game, deliberately small.
  • Builds revenue to fund what comes next.
  • Mainly focused on a fun gameplay experience.

Requires Talent · World IP

  • Writer for World IP
  • Concept Artist for World IP
Game 2 Bigger scope

The sequel that solidifies the IP

  • Bigger scope — solidifies the World IP.
  • Sequel of the Game 1 World IP.
  • Focus on world building and making it emotionally compelling.

Higher funding required, but generally higher revenue (research?). Revenue from Battlepass / Cosmetics / Sponsorships.

Core Gameplay

The game loop — a simple boss rush.

Regular Boss → Upgrades → Regular Boss → Upgrades → Regular Boss → Upgrades → Area Boss

1 Area = 1 Run. A very simple boss rush: clear three regular bosses (collecting upgrades between each) and finish the run on the area boss. Proving this loop is the whole point of the Early Prototype — the playable classes and normal bosses can stand in as temporary Asset Store assets for now; the loop is what matters.
